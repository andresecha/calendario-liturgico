About this calculator

This tool computes the key dates of the Christian liturgical year for any year you enter, in both the Julian and Gregorian calendars. Enter a year (from 532 onward) to obtain the date of Easter and every movable feast that depends on it.

Calculated dates include the Epiphany, the Baptism of the Lord, Ash Wednesday and the season of Lent, Palm Sunday, Holy Thursday, Good Friday and Holy Saturday, Easter Sunday, the Ascension, Pentecost, Trinity Sunday, Corpus Christi, Christ the King, the four Sundays of Advent, and Christmas.

It is intended for historians and researchers who work with documents dated by liturgical feasts rather than by civil dates. All calculations run entirely in your browser.
